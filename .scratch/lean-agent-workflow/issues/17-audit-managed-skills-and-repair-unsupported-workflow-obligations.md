## What to build

Audit one identified snapshot of the installed self-generated managed skill library and give every member a supported disposition: retain, narrow, consolidate, or propose retirement. Repair unsupported workflow obligations within the approved boundaries, while preserving genuinely useful safeguards.

Inspect trigger breadth, authority conflicts, duplicate procedures, mandatory resets/rechecks, and incident-specific rules generalized without evidence. Distinguish current wording, actual historical loading, and measured execution cost. The session-efficiency audit guidance is a concrete candidate: a compaction size reduction does not establish that a session lasted too long, and continue-first remains the approved context policy.

## Placement / boundaries

“The managed-skill audit is one bounded workstream over an identified installed-library snapshot, not a required stage for every task.”

Own edits only in the managed library and its affected references, using the managed-skill interface. Do not broadly rewrite unrelated user-authored/plugin skills, override a still-binding harness rule, or weaken security/platform/acceptance obligations. Issues 02, 10, 11, and 12 own their named isolation, landing, publication, and merge repairs: link those dispositions rather than making competing edits or turning this audit into their prerequisite.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Identify the bounded installed-library snapshot and account for each member; do not present a sample as a comprehensive review or chase newly generated skills indefinitely.
- [ ] Produce one compact disposition record with source evidence, coverage limits, and historical-load evidence where available. Current presence is not proof a skill caused historical overhead.
- [ ] Apply evidence-backed repairs not owned by the named sibling issues, migrate affected references, and check for intervening changes before updating shared managed content.
- [ ] Evaluate representative workflow decisions: dependent work does not require a gratuitous reset, applicable proof does not require blanket replay, name overrides do not bypass actual authority, and binding security/native proof remains required.
- [ ] An unresolved contradiction is not reported as repaired. Findings that would change approved safety/acceptance boundaries are recorded for a separate decision, not silently applied.
- [ ] Keep this a one-off audit and targeted repair, not another mandatory per-task stage, global model/compaction change, or new universal checklist. Count removed duplicate obligations and observed unnecessary actions without deriving runtime savings from shorter wording.
